## Machine conditions

Native macOS arm64 with 8 GB RAM, Compose running, cached model weights only,
`caffeinate -i` active to prevent idle sleep. Warm-up model loading is excluded.
Pre-existing swap usage was about 6.7 GB and rose to about 8.9 GB after dev; these are
qualified machine observations, not a clean-machine performance claim. Applications
were not closed. No lint or pytest jobs ran concurrently with these measurements.
The old bge top-50 timing remains invalid; this run measures MiniLM and does not
provide a new bge latency comparison.

[Recorded before/after load, swap, thermal reports and pinned model revisions](2026-11-07_m04s4_conditions.json).

## Decision made on dev before the test run

Retain locked D7 and MiniLM; plan top-20 for the future answer path. Rerank does not
show a clear quality improvement: its P@5 and MRR confidence intervals overlap hybrid
heavily. Top-50 costs more latency without a clear quality benefit. This retains the
locked architecture, without claiming rerank beats hybrid. Parent expansion and
`final_k` remain M5-S2. No retrieval parameters or labels were changed.

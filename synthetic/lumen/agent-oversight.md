# Agent Oversight Program

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref LMP-CANARY-N6YD

## Purpose

This program explains how Lumen Pay selects, trains, and monitors the retail agents that accept cash for its payment service.

## Onboarding an agent

Before signing, the Partner Risk team checks the agent's business registration, ownership, and adverse media, and confirms that the agent has a named compliance contact.

## Training

Agent staff complete compliance training before their first transaction and once every 12 months. An agent with overdue training is suspended after 30 days.

## Monitoring

| Measure | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Transactions just under the ID limit | More than 15 percent of volume | Partner Risk review |
| One sender using many recipients | 10 recipients in 7 days | Financial Crimes alert |
| Complaints | 3 in a calendar quarter | Mystery shop visit |

## Audits and termination

Every agent receives an on-site audit once every 24 months, and high-risk agents once every 12 months. An agent that hides activity or ignores a corrective plan is terminated.

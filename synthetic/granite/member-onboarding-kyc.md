# Member Onboarding and KYC Standard

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref GRC-CANARY-D4QS

## Purpose

This standard describes how Granite Ridge Credit Union confirms who a new member is and assesses membership risk.

## Eligibility and identity

A member service representative confirms field-of-membership eligibility, then collects name, date of birth, address, and an identification number. Identity is confirmed before the share account is funded.

## Member risk tiers

| Tier | Example | Refresh cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Employee of a sponsor company | Every 48 months |
| Elevated | Member-owned small business | Every 18 months |
| Enhanced | Cash-heavy business or politically exposed person | Every 6 months |

## Business members

For a legal entity member, staff identify each individual who owns 25 percent or more of the equity interests and one individual who controls the entity.

## Enhanced due diligence

Enhanced tier members require a written source-of-funds statement and an expected activity profile.

**EDD approver:** Chief Risk Officer

The membership stays restricted to deposits only until the EDD approver signs the profile.

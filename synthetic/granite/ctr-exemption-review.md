# Currency Transaction Report Exemption Review

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref GRC-CANARY-F2TK

## Purpose

This procedure governs how Granite Ridge Credit Union decides which members may be treated as exempt persons for currency transaction reporting.

## Reporting rule

A Currency Transaction Report (CTR) is required for currency transactions of more than $10,000 by, through, or to the credit union, and it must be filed within 15 calendar days after the transaction. Transactions are aggregated across all branches.

## Exemption requests

A branch manager may propose a business member for exemption. The compliance team checks that the member meets the regulatory criteria and that its cash activity fits its business.

| Step | Owner | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal and activity summary | Branch manager | Any time |
| Eligibility review | Compliance analyst | Within 20 business days |
| Approval | Compliance Committee | Next monthly meeting |

## Annual review

Every exempt member is reviewed each January. The compliance team removes the exemption when the member stops meeting the criteria, and exemption never removes the duty to report suspicious activity.

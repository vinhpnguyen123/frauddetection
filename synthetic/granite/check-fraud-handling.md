# Check Fraud Handling

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref GRC-CANARY-Z5NM

## Purpose

This procedure explains how Granite Ridge Credit Union detects and responds to altered, counterfeit, and stolen checks.

## Hold and review

**Internal review threshold:** $15,000

Any deposited check at or above the internal review threshold is reviewed by the Deposit Operations team before funds become available. Lower amounts are reviewed when the check is out of pattern for the member.

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Payee name looks altered or washed | Contact the paying institution |
| Check deposited into an account under 60 days old | Extended hold |
| Mobile deposit followed by quick withdrawal | Freeze remaining funds and call the member |

## Mail theft

Stolen and altered checks are often taken from the mail. For a suspected mail theft case, the compliance team includes the key term FIN-2023-MAILTHEFT in the SAR, as FinCEN requests in its 2023 alert, and tells the member how to report the theft to the U.S. Postal Inspection Service.

## Member losses

Member claims are logged within one business day and decided within ten business days.

# Wire Transfer Review Thresholds

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref BCB-CANARY-J8LN

## Purpose

This standard sets the internal review levels for wire transfers sent or received by Bluefin Community Bank customers. These levels are internal controls, not regulatory reporting thresholds.

## Review levels

**Internal review threshold:** $25,000

Any single outgoing wire at or above the internal review threshold is held for review by a wire operations supervisor before release.

| Wire type | Additional control |
|---|---|
| Domestic, first wire to a new beneficiary | Call back to the customer on a known number |
| International, any amount | Sanctions screening and purpose of payment recorded |
| International, at or above the threshold | BSA analyst reviews before release |

## Fraud checks

Staff stop and escalate a wire when the customer seems to be following instructions from a third party, when the beneficiary details changed by email, or when the wire empties a long-held account.

## Cut-off

Wires held for review after 3:00 p.m. Eastern are released the next business day.

# Politically Exposed Person Approval Matrix

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref BCB-CANARY-M9VC

## Purpose

This matrix sets who at Bluefin Community Bank must approve a relationship with a politically exposed person (PEP) or a close associate of one.

## Identifying a PEP

Staff screen each new customer against the bank's PEP list at onboarding and every quarter. A match is confirmed or cleared by a BSA analyst within five business days.

## Approval levels

| Relationship | First approval | Final approval |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic PEP, deposit account only | Branch manager | BSA Officer |
| Foreign PEP, any product | BSA Officer | Chief Executive Officer |
| Close associate of a foreign PEP | BSA Officer | Chief Executive Officer |
| Any PEP requesting a commercial loan | Chief Lending Officer | Board Risk Committee |

**EDD approver:** BSA Officer

## Conditions

Every approved PEP relationship is rated high and reviewed every 12 months. Being a PEP is not by itself a reason to decline an account or to file a report; the decision depends on the risk of the relationship.

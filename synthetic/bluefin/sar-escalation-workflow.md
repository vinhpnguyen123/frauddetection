# Suspicious Activity Escalation Workflow

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref BCB-CANARY-H6TW

## Purpose

This workflow explains how a concern raised by any Bluefin Community Bank employee reaches a decision on filing a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR).

## Raising a concern

Any employee who notices unusual activity submits an Unusual Activity Referral form. Employees must not tell the customer about the referral.

**SAR escalation window:** 3 business days

The referring employee's branch manager must pass the referral to the BSA team within the escalation window, counted from the day the concern was raised.

## Investigation

1. A BSA analyst opens a case and reviews twelve months of account activity.
2. The analyst records a recommendation to file or not to file.
3. The SAR Review Committee meets every Tuesday and decides on open cases.

## Filing deadlines

A SAR must be filed no later than 30 calendar days from the initial detection of facts that may be a basis for filing. If no suspect can be identified, the period extends to 60 days. The bank tracks these dates in the case log.

## Records

Copies of SARs and supporting documentation are retained for five years from the date of filing. A decision not to file is documented in the case with its reason.

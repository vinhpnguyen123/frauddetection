# Customer Due Diligence and Enhanced Due Diligence Procedure

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref BCB-CANARY-R2KD

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how Bluefin Community Bank understands its customers, builds a customer risk profile, and decides when enhanced due diligence (EDD) applies.

## Customer risk rating

Every customer receives a rating at account opening. Relationship managers complete the rating form, and the BSA team reviews every rating above low.

| Rating | Typical customer | Review cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Local retail customer with payroll deposits | Every 36 months |
| Medium | Local small business with stable activity | Every 24 months |
| High | Cash-intensive business, foreign correspondent, or politically exposed person | Every 12 months |

## Legal entity customers

Staff collect beneficial ownership information for each legal entity customer: every individual who owns 25 percent or more of the equity interests, and one individual with significant responsibility to control the entity.

## Enhanced due diligence

EDD applies to every high-rated customer. The file must record the source of funds, the expected monthly activity, and the reason for the relationship.

**EDD approver:** BSA Officer

No high-rated account may be opened or renewed until the EDD approver signs the file.

## Ongoing monitoring

Actual activity is compared with expected activity each month. A variance of more than 40 percent for two months in a row triggers an early review.

# Cash-Intensive Business Review

Fictional document for software testing. Not the policy of any real institution.

Policy ref BCB-CANARY-P3XF

## Purpose

This standard describes how Bluefin Community Bank reviews customers whose businesses handle large amounts of currency, such as convenience stores, car washes, and restaurants.

## Onboarding

The relationship manager visits the business in person before the account opens and records the expected monthly cash deposits. The site visit report stays in the customer file.

## Monthly review

The BSA team compares actual cash deposits with the expected amount for each cash-intensive customer.

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Within 25 percent of expected | No action |
| 25 to 50 percent above expected | Relationship manager asks the customer why |
| More than 50 percent above expected | BSA analyst opens a case |

## Structuring indicators

Analysts look for repeated deposits just under the currency reporting level, deposits split across branches on the same day, and sudden changes in deposit pattern. Banks must report currency transactions of more than $10,000, and multiple transactions are aggregated for that purpose.

## Annual visit

Each cash-intensive customer receives one unannounced site visit per calendar year.
